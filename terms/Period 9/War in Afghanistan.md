## Section 1

War fought against the Taliban and AL Qaeda in Afghanistan following the attacks on September 11, 2001. This was the longest war in United States history going on 20 years. It was called Operation enduring freedom. The reason that this war started was because Al-Qaeda refused to surrender their leader Osama Bin Laden. This war ended with  the U.S. military leaving Afghanistan in 2021 after the collapse of the Afghan government. In the end 2,300 U.S. military death, and an estimated financial cost of $2.3 trillion.
