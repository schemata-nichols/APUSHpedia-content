> Quakers are members of the Religious Society of Friends, a 17th Century religious Protestant movement (originating in England).

## Section 1

Perhaps the most central idea of Quakerism is the notion that everyone has an 'Inner Light', given by god, that grants them a sense of his oversight. Rejecting typical religious procedure, Quakers commonly await and listen for the word of god to guide them. In addition, there is no widely accepted central authority which governs it. Quakerism quickly spread throughout England in the 17th and 18th century, but persecution there forced many to flee to North America. Pennsylvania, founded by William Penn, became the largest haven for Quakers.
