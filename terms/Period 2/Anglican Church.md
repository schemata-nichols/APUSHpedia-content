> State Church of England created by Henry the VIII to legalize his divorce to Catherine of Aragon.

## Section 1

During the Great Schism when many rulers and people were splitting from the Catholic Church, England remained loyal to the Vatican. However, this all changed when the Pope denied Henry VIII to divorce Catherine of Aragon in the early 1530s. King Henry VIII split England's ties with the Catholic faith and started the Reformation in England, creating a new branch of Christianity: Anglicanism. This is also known as the Church of England. The Anglican Church took on many Protestant ways, such as preaching in English, but held on to the Catholic hierarchy system. Tensions began to build in England between Catholics, Puritans and Anglicans, which directly led to the English Civil War in 1642. Throughout the world, as England expanded its colonial empire, Anglican Churches were built.
