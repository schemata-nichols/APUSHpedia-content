> Born in New York in 1807, Solomon Northup was a free black man who was a skilled carpenter and accomplished violinist. However, in 1841 two white men drugged and captured him in Washington DC and stole his papers that made him a free man. Then, they sold him into the slave trade. He was sold to a plantation owner in Louisiana, where he worked for twelve years in cotton and sugar mills. With the help of a Canadian Carpenter named Samuel Bass, Northup was able to get letters sent to his family in the north. New York officials intervened in 1853 and got Northup back from his enslavement using a law that protected free African Americans from being kidnapped and sold into slavery. When he returned home, Solomon Northup wrote and published his book 12 Years a Slave.
