## Section 1

Cyrus McCormick (1089-1884) was an American inventor and businessman best known for developing the mechanical reaper, a horse-drawn machine that made harvesting grain faster and easier. He successfully demonstrated his reaper in 1831 and patented it in 1834. In 1847, he established a factory in Chicago to manufacture the machines, helping expand agricultural production and industrial growth. His invention reduced the labor needed to harvest crops and helped farmers produced more food. His company later became part of the International Harvester.
