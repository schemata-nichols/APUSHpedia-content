> The Napoleonic wars was a influential event that spanned the course of 1803-1815 that affected the [[The United States of America|US|]] in a variety of ways in relation to Europe including trade, alliance, and conflict. This war, fought primarily in Europe, stemmed to the war of 1812 including a wide variety of factors like impressment. It also caused the [[Louisiana Purchase]] majorly when France wanted to sell this territory.

## Subtitle 1

The Napoleonic wars (1803-1815) was a European conflict involving the French leader Napoleons rein and wars on surrounding territories like the British or Prussians. Napoleon was known primarily in Europe for his devastating war tactics and utilization of his loyal regiments like the old guard. This pinned Britain and other European countries into states of panic as well as stressing France which influenced their deals with the United states as it was a foreign war they had to somewhat get involved with. This is primarily seen in the war of 1812 & the Louisiana Purchase. 

## Subtitle 2

The United States during this time was widely neutral to the conflict equally trading to France and Britain for its commodities while steering clear from the general conflict. But, the United States was still affected by efforts of the British to garner more troops. This including [[impressment]] on traders from the United states to serve in the British army which angered the United states as it was forceful enslavement of their people which where widely neutral. This led to the United states declaring the war of 1812 after other efforts like the embargo act of 1807 to initially just halt trade with the British. They declared [[the War of 1812]] to try to get justice for all the impressed sailors. This ended with the Treaty of Ghent which was widely neutral to both sides and got most of the impressed sailors back while restoring any loss or gain of territory between the British and United States. 

## Subtitle 3

Another influence was the Louisiana Purchase which was made by Napoleon. He primarily did this to fund a urgent war with Great Britain as he focused his general efforts into Europe versus North America. In order to fund this effort he low balled the price to the land to try to get Jefferson to agree to purchase it with haste as it would gave the US a huge land advantage while providing France with money for the war. Even after constitutional struggles internally with Jeffersons ideals, this worked as he agreed to purchase it despite the constitution not explicitly stating the presidents right to do so. Without this urgent requirement for funds due to the Napoleonic Wars the Louisiana Purchase was likely to never occur and the United states would still be bordering this French territory for much longer. It also would not spark rapid colonization of the west or other major movements in relation to the territory got from the Louisiana Purchase.
