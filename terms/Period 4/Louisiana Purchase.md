> The Louisiana Purchase was a massive 1803 land purchase from France that doubled the size of the United States, and heightened its economic and political power.

## Section 1

Tight on money for an impending war with Great Britain, Napoleon Bonaparte had given up hope for a North American colonial empire. To gain funds, Napoleon agreed to sell the Louisiana territory, land covering the Western Mississippi Basin, from France to the United States. Perhaps the largest land bargain in history, the U.S. acquired 2,144,520 square km for a price of just 3 cents per acre. The deal was so good that Jefferson briefly abandoned his stance as a strict constructionist. However, the Louisiana Purchase Treaty did not ask for the consent of the Native American nations living on the land.
