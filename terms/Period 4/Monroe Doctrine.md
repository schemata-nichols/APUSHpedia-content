> President James Monroe announced the Monroe Doctrine in 1823. The policy warned European countries not to create new colonies or interfere with independent countries found in the Americas. In return, the United States said that it would stay out of European conflicts. This matters because it shows the U.S. would stop European countries from taking more land in the Americas.
