> The Second Great Awakening was a religious revival movement that began in the 1790s and spread throughout the United States during the early 1800s. ## Section 1

The Second Great Awakening was a religious revival movement that began in the late 1700s and grew during the early 1800s. It encouraged people to improve society and fix social problems. It helped inspire reform movements such as abolitionism, temperance, and women's rights.
