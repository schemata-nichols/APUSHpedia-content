## Submission by Leonardo Kelly

> The Second Great Awakening was a religious revival movement that began in the 1790s and spread throughout the United States during the early 1800s. It encouraged people to improve themselves and society. It helped inspire social reform movements such as abolitionism, temperance, and women's rights. These movements aimed to fix problems in American society.

### Section 1

The Second Great Awakening was a religious revival movement that began in the late 1700s and grew during the early 1800s. It encouraged people to improve society and fix social problems. It helped inspire reform movements such as abolitionism, temperance, and women's rights.

## Submission by Mia Reich

> A religious revival in the early 1800s that urged people to repent and take charge of their own journey. It made ordinary people feel they could improve themselves, and many believed they could improve society too.

### Section 1

The Second Great Awakening happened in the early 1800s, mostly between the 1790s and 1840s. It started because many Americans wanted a more personal and emotional kind of religion, rather than the strict churches of the time. Huge outdoor revival meetings, called camp meetings, drew thousands of people, especially on the frontier. The revivals changed American society. Many people believed that if they could be saved, society could be made better too, so they started reform movements. These included abolition, temperance, better schooling, and women's rights. Women made up a large part of the church community and took leadership roles in these causes, which gave them a way to be active in public life.
