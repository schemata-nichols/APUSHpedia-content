> A list of accomplishments and achievements completed by John Quincy Adams through his life

## Section 1

Some things to note before jumping into the article is:  
JQA is the oldest son of President John Adams. John Quincy Adams was a part of the Federalist Party and was a part of the National Republican Party.  

## Section 2
  
JQA was Secretary of State to James Monroe (1817-1825) and also significantly helped with the Monroe Doctrine. He was the 6th President of the United States (1825-1829). During the election of 1824, Andrew Jackson won the most popular and electoral votes; however, since neither candidate won the majority, the House of Representatives decided that JQA would be the 6th President of the United States. Henry Clay was the Speaker of the House at the time, and it was Clay’s support and influence that helped sway the House of Representatives to choose JQA for the win. Shortly after, JQA made Henry Clay the Secretary of State, which made Jackson’s supporters angry, and it was called the “corrupt bargain.” This chain of events led Jackson’s supporters to form the Democratic Party in 1828, which helped Jackson win against JQA for President in 1829. This rise in democracy led to the formation of the Second Party System (Democrats v. Republicans). As President, JQA had a nationalist agenda including federally funded roads and canals, a national university, and a focus on scientific developments. Formed the American System (created a more modern US economy). He was a US Congressman (1831-1848). JQA was also a representative from Massachusetts and fought against the expansion of slavery  

## Section 3
  
JQA’s election was a turning point in American political culture. His popularity declined during his presidency, not only because of the “corrupt bargain,” but also because he supported Native Americans against the demands of westward settlers and the Tariff of Abominations.
