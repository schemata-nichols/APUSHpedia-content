> Thomas Jefferson was the 3rd president of the United States. Sworn into office in 1801, Jefferson was the first elected president to be a part of the democratic republican party.

## Section 1

Thomas Jefferson wrote the famous Declaration of Independence shortly after attending the second Continental Congress. Under his presidency, Jefferson maintained that the constitution must be adhered to absolutely, he did, however, participate in the Louisiana Purchase in 1803 which was not within the scope of his powers. Still, Jefferson’s acquisition of the Louisiana territory from France was key to the nation’s foundation, leading to westward expansion and disputes about slavery in new states.
