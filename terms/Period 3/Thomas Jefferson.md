## Section 1

Thomas Jefferson, the 3rd president of the Untied states, is primarily known for drafting the declaration of independence in 1776 following the Second Contienal Congress. He also purchased the lousiana territory from France (aka the Lousiana Purchase) in 1803, which led to gradual westward expansion, disputes about slavery in new states, and gradual emacipation.
