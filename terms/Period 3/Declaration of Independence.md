> The Declaration of Independence was signed on July 4, 1776, and claimed that the thirteen American colonies were separating from Great Britain. Primarily written by Thomas Jefferson, it argued that people have natural rights (including life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness). The document, influenced by Enlightenment ideas, complained against King George III. The Declaration helped to justify the American Revolution and established ideas about freedom and equality that proceeded to influence America for the years following.
