## Section 1

The Northwest Ordinance was passed in 1787 under the Articles of Confederation. It established a process for western territories to become states equal to the original states. It also banned slavery in the Northwest Territory and encouraged public education. The law helped the United States expand westward and maintain order in its new territories.
