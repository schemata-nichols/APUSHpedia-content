> In 1793, Philadelphia experienced a horrible epidemic which led to the death of many colonists, it was called Yellow Fever. Yellow Fever is a virus that was spread by the Aedes aegypti mosquitoes from Africa, they were likely spread to the Western Hemisphere through slave trade in the 1600’s. The virus was also likely to be in the U.S. water barrels via ships. These mosquitoes would sting one person and it would spread to many others that the statistic became “For every 1 case of severe infection there are between 1 and 70 infections that are asymptomatic or mild” (Prinzi, 2021). As the fever spread, the white residents started to flee the villages thus leaving the enslaved people and other white people in the village.  Richard Allen and Abasalom Jones were two enslaved men became the leaders of the community and were doing everything they could to help suppress the Yellow Fever, and even though they helped contain the yellow fever they were still discriminated against and told that they werent helping and just making this fever worse. These negative accusations toward black people despite their hard work sparked anger in Richard and Abasalom, thus leading ot the creation of the “The Life, Experience, and Gospel Labours of the Rt. Rev. Richard Allen. To Which is Annexed the Rise and Progress of the African Methodist Episcopal Church in the United States of America. Containing a Narrative of the Yellow Fever in the Year of Our Lord 1793: With an Address to the People of Colour in the United States” (Allen, 1831). This narrative is a direct reflection of the injustice that happened even when black people were helping out more than the white people throughout this horrible pandemic. This narrative later became famous and is one of the many primary sources of black people standing up against racial prejudice.

## Section 1

The Yellow Fever Epidemic of 1793 was a direct reflection of social hierarchy, racial prejudice, and the idea of abolitionism. The Yellow Fever Epidemic was a horrible disease that killed many people but also a time where black people were the heroes and still weren't treated like one. The white residents who had both a social and racial hierarchy against the black people was shown pretty clearly toward their attitudes toward Richard Allen and Absalom Jones when they were some of the leaders throughout this era. Then the creation of their narrative reflected how they were treated and the idea of abolitionism to black slaves would start come up. in oycnclsuoin the Yellow Fever was a prime example of how black slaves were treated regardless of the situation they were in.
