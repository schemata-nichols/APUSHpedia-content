> The first constitution of the United States. Created in 1781 and replaced in 1787 by the actual U.S Constitution.

## Section 1

The Articles of Confederation were the first actual Constitution of United States. Signed in 1781 following the Second Continental Congress, the Articles of Confederation focused on all 13 states having individual government, which ensured freedom for each state and less of a monarchy. However, this constitution did not account for federal tax, so the federal government became poor and weak compared to state governments. Because of these issues the Articles were replaced by the modern day US Constitution in 1787 at the Constitutional Convention.
