> The Articles of Confederation were the first constitution of the United States. Its ratification in all 13 states took place in 1781.

## Section 1

The Articles of Confederation, being the first constitution of the United States, came with its pros and cons. First, an avoidance of anything resembling monarchical rule meant that each state had its own government rather than a powerful federal government. However, this meant that the federal government lacked the power to collect taxes, weakening its capacity. Additionally, unlike the current United States constitution, congress only had one body, in which each state received one vote regardless of population. Good luck amending the constitution because all 13 states had to agree for an amendment to be passed. And so, it was not amended–they scrapped it– replacing it with the constitution we have today.
