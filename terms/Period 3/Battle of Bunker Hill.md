> The Battle of Bunker Hill in 1775 was the first major battle of the American Revolutionary War that resulted in a British victory, but with more British casualties. The battle strengthened Patriots morals and proved that they could hold their own against the British Army.

## Section 1

The Battle of Bunker Hill was on June 17th, 1775, is known as the first major battle of the American Revolutionary War.

## Section 2

British Commander-In-Chief General Sir Thomas Gage implemented a strategy, in which the British Army created plans to launch attacks on America’s North and South Boston. However, the details were leaked, and in response, around midnight on June 16th, hundreds of colonial soldiers used tools such as pickaxes to build a fort on Breed’s Hill. The battle was in Charlestown and consisted of ~3,000 British soldiers and ~2,200 American soldiers, notably, among the defenders were African-Americans, both freed and enslaved.

## Section 3

The battle ended in a British victory, yet, 1,054 there were British soldiers casualties, compared to the 2,200 American Patriots. Even with the loss, the Patriots morals remained high, and the battle proved that the Americans could hold their own against the powerful British army.
