> Inflation is when prices of goods, services, and overall living rise over time due to many factors. It makes the money worth less giving the people less purchasing power with their standard wages. A big reason for big swings of inflation is supply shocks which can happen because of war or natural disasters. The good or service becomes so desirable that the price begins rising.

## Section 1

Inflation has been seen all across time for reasons that are very different. It can be due to the government overprinting money, or natural disasters which make certain goods and services more desirable. A key point of inflation for APUSH is due to the American Revolution. The war lasted from 1775 to 1783 and created immense demand for many goods. The Continental Congress actually wasn't able to tax the people in the U.S. yet because they only had the power to request funds from the states. This led them to have to print tons of money to be able to fund the war which led to hyperinflation. Hyperinflation is an extreme version of normal inflation, that just grows rapidly. By 1778 inflation rose to around 30% of normal prices which is just staggering.
