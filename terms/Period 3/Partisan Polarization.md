> Partisan polarization is when political parties become divided because they disagree on government policies. During the Election of 1800, Thomas Jefferson’s Democratic-Republicans and John Adams’s Federalists disagreed over federal power and the economy.

## Section 1

Partisan polarization increased in the 1790s as Federalists and Democratic-Republicans disagreed over the power of the federal government, the national bank, and the economy. These disagreements led to political conflict during the Election of 1800 between John Adams and Thomas Jefferson. Jefferson won the election, showing a peaceful transfer of power between opposing political parties.
