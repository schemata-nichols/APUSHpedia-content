> The first national bank for the federal government. Proposed by Alexander Hamilton in 1790 and enacted in 1791.

## Section 1

The First National Bank was propsed and designed to allow the federal governemnt to have actual tax revenue. Prior to the creation of the bank, the federal government had to rely on tariffs and individual state's money. This made the federal government exceptionally weak compared to state governments, which had major repercussions. Shay's rebellion, a revolt in western Massachusetts could not be resolved by the federal government because the government had no money to pay troops to clear the militia of revolters, so ultimately the wealthy landowners of Massachusetts had to pay for another militia to deal with the revolters. This showcased how poor and weak the federal government really was.
