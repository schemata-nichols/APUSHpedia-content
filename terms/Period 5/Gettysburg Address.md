> The Gettysburg Address was a speech delivered by President Lincoln on November 19, 1863, at the dedication of cemetery for Union soldiers killed at the Battle of Gettysburg.

## Section 1

His speech connected Civil War to the founding principles of the US, specifically equality and democracy. He argued that the nation needed to continue fighting so that the sacrafices of dead soldier would not be wasted. He described the war as a test of whether a government based on the people could survive. The Gettysburg Address redefined the meaning of civil war by emphasizing equality, unity, and democracy. It also shape the idea that the war could lead to a new birth of freedom for the country.
