> The Emancipation Proclamation was an executive order issued by President Lincoln on January 1, 1863, during the civil war.

## Section 1

The proclamation declared enslaved people in areas rebelling against the Union freedom. However, it didn't immediately abolish slavery throughout the country because it didnt apply to certain states under Union control. It allowed Black men to serve in Union military. This changed the purpose of Civil War by making ending slavery a goal instead of preserving the Union. The Emancipation Proclamation strengthened the Union's postion, weakened the Confederacy, and paved the way for the 13th Amendment.
