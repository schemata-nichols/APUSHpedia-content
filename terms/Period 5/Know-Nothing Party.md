> Mid 19th century political party that opposed immigrants and Catholics.

## Section 1

Know- Nothing Party was a Nativist, Anti Catholic third party organized in 1854 in reaction to a large-scale German and Irish immigration; the party's only presidential candidate was murdered for more in 1856
