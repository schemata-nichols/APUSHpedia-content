> The Kansas-Nebraska Act by Stephan A. Douglas a U.S senator gave his idea of popular sovereignty to the states. This allowed settlers to vote on whether slavery would be legal in the new states/territories. Douglass wanted to organize the territories and build western settlement. Caused violent conflicts between pro and anti slavery settlers. This helped lead to the formation of the Republican Party.

## Section 1

Passed in 1854, the Kansas-Nebraska act gave Stephan A. Douglas's idea of popular sovereignty to states. Meaning setters of a territory/state were able to decide whether slavery would be allowed within a new states borders. This overturned the Missouri Compromise use of Latitude as the border for pro-slavery, and anti-slavery states.

## Section 2

In response, a group on antislavery congressman made the Appeal of Independent Democrats. They proclaimed Douglas's bill as a "gross violation of a sacred pledge," it convinced millions of northerners that southern leaders wanted nothing less than expansion of slavery throughout the new west. Converting the free territory into a land inhabited of Slaves and Masters. This brought much violance between anti and pro slavery groups. Leading to Bleeding Kansas

## Section 3

Through Douglas's leadership the Kansas-Nebraska Act became a law in 1854. Shattering the democratic parties unity and created a strong reorganization of U.S Politics. Parties such as the Whig party, folded relatively quickly through crisis. The south became heavily democratic, and former whigs joining a new party, know as the Republican Party, focusing on preventing the expansion of Slavery.
