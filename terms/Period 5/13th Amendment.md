> The 13th Amendment to the U.S constitution abolished slavery throughout the US in 1865.

## Section 1

Congress passed the amendment on January 31, 1865, and it was ratifed December 6, 1865. Unlike emancipation Proclaimation, which applied to only certian areas during the civil war, the 13th amendment abolished slavery nationwide, execpt as punishment for a crime following conviction. The 13th Amendment was a major achievement of reconstruction because it legally ended slavery. However it didnt guarentee equal rights or eliminate discrimination.
