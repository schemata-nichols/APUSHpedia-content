> Bartolome de las Casas was a Catholic priest who spoke out against the Spanish of mistreating and forcibly converting Native Americans.

## Section 1

Bartolome de las Casas wrote "A Brief Relation of the Destruction of the Indies" in 1552, describing the brutal conduct towards the Natives of the Caribbean by the Spanish. Las Casas argued that the Spanish had no right to take the land and liberties of the Natives, declaring that the entire human race is one.
