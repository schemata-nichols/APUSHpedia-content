## Section 1

The Columbian Exchange was the trading of goods to/from the old and new world after Christopher Columus' voyage to the new world. This exchange gave the old world things such as new cash crops e.g. Corn, Potatoes, sweet potatoes, cassava, and etc; new animals such as turkeys and llamas. The new food brought the life expectancy of people from the old world up exponentially. The new world got animals such as horses, cattle, pigs, chickens, and etc; they also got crops such as wheat, barley, oats, and etc; although they got many goods from this trade there were also many diseases that came to the new world and killed many Native Americans such as smallpox, measles, influenza, and etc.
