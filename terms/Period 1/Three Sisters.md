## Section 1

The Three Sisters was a farming method used by Native Americans long before European colonization. It involved growing corn, beans, and squash together because each crop helped the others grow. The corn provided support for the beans to climb, the beans added nutrients to the soil, and the squash covered the ground to keep moisture in and reduce weeds. This method helped Native Americans grow food more efficiently and maintain a reliable food supply. It also allowed many Native American communities to establish permanent settlements, support larger populations, and develop stronger agricultural societies. The Three Sisters farming method showed how Native Americans adapted to their environment and used natural resources to support their communities.
