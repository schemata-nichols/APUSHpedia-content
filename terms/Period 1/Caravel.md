> A caravel was a type of ship mostly used by the Spanish and Portuguese, initially used for fishing, but eventually facilitated long journeys to the Americas. They were faster than most vessels at the time and popular for cargo shipping. They were exceptionally successful at the time because of their lateen sails and how low they sat in the water. Notable caravels are Christopher Columbus’, the ships that made his grand trip to the Americas.

## Section 1

The invention of the caravel led to important trade and the start of colonization in the Americas. The initial motivation for its creation was the Indian ocean trade, The Columbian Exchange primarily used caravel ships as well, which then led to the spread of goods, ideas, people, animals, plants, and ideas between the Americas and the Old World.
