This is a practical, page-level coverage set for APUSHpedia. The College Board publishes required course content, not an official vocabulary list; therefore, “all terms” here means the set of standalone pages needed to explain that required content with usable historical evidence.

## Classification

Use `apush-priority`, not `apush-relevance`:

| Priority | Editorial meaning | Typical reading time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Foundational or recurring; essential to understanding several topics or a major period | 4–5 min |
| 4 | Major required development and broadly useful evidence | 3–4 min |
| 3 | Supporting concept, event, person, law, or institution | 2–3 min |
| 2 | Specific illustrative evidence worth its own short page | 1–2 min |
| 1 | Optional enrichment; not part of required coverage | No required page |

Terms are assigned one primary home only. A term belongs in **Long-term** when it remains analytically useful across at least three periods; otherwise, it belongs to the period where it is introduced or most important. Priority is an editorial judgment, not a prediction of whether a term will appear on a particular exam.
